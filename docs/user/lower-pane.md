# The Lower Pane

A project with a very large Backlog or Inbox does not fit well beside the columns you work in. On the desktop you can
move such a column into a **lower pane**: it takes the full width at the bottom of the board, and your regular columns
stay in the top half.

## Moving a column

Open the column's settings (the **…** button in its header, or double-click its title) and tick **Lower pane**. Untick it to take the column back to the board.
You can put several columns in the lower pane, and they share its width. The cards of a docked column flow into as many
columns as fit, so a long list shows many cards at once.

Cards can be dragged between the panes like between any two columns, and the columns of each pane can be dragged into
another order.

## The size of the pane

Drag the divider between the panes up or down. With the divider focused, the arrow keys up and down change the size in
steps. The pane takes between 15 % and 80 % of the board's height, and Jotter remembers the size.

## Good to know

- The setting belongs to this browser, not to the vault: it is not saved in the Markdown files, and another device or
  browser starts with the regular board.
- The pane is for one project at a time. In the **All Projects** view and on a narrow screen every column stays in the
  board.
- A column keeps its place in the order of the project, whichever pane shows it.
